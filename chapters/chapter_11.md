Lifecycle of a Frog
A frog goes through four stages of life. As the frog grows, it changes. This is
called metamorphosis. The four stages of a frog's life are :
Egg- Female frogs lay eggs. This is also called spawning. Tadpoles
hatch from
these eggs. It takes from one to three weeks for the eggs to hatch.
Tadpole- A tadpole is born with a tail but no legs. The tadpole lives in water. It
breathes with gills. Later on the tadpole develops legs.
Froglet- A tadpole grows into a froglet. This froglet has developed legs for
hopping. It now has lungs which help it breathe.
Frog- A froglet grows into an adult frog. And adult frog has no tail. A new life cycle
begins when a female frog lays eggs.
