Sachin Tendulkar
Sachin Tendulkar, one of the greatest cricketers of
all time, is a proof of the power of talent combined
with dedication and hard work. He was born in
Mumbai on 24th April 1973. His father, Ramesh
Tendulkar, was a novelist and mother, Rajni
Tendulkar, was a home-maker. He started
playing cricket when he was 11 and very soon his
name spread all around as a cricket prodigy.
But success did not come to him easily. He
belonged to a middle-class family and could not
•
afford any luxury. He had to work very hard all through the day to juggle between
his cricket practice and school. Every day before and after school he went for his
cricket coaching by bus. He also owned only one cricket uniform for practice. So
after his coaching, he also had to wash it himself for wearing it the next time. On
many occasions the inseams were still wet when he wore them for practice. But he did
not let these hiccups pull him away from his passion and did not let his practice
suffer because of these problems.
Ramakant Achrekar spotted his exceptional talent and coached him initially. But
Tendulkar still consulted him even after becoming an icon. This shows his humble
nature and simplicity.
Sachin Tendulkar has played many inspiring and winning innings for Team India and
has achieved many personal milestones also. He has received Arjun Award, Rajiv
Gandhi Khel Ratna Award and Padma Vibhushan and many other awards. His crowning
glory came in 2014, when he became the youngest and the first sports person ever to
receive the Bharat Ratna, India's highest Civilian honour. Now that he is retired, he
is actively i volved with many NGOs and works for various social causes/
