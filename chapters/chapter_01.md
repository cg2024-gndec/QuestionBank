Ping also got a royal seed. He took great care of the seed day and night for the
next six months. But not a single leaf sprouted from the seed. He tried and tried
but nothing came off it. Finally the D-Day arrived. He was hesitant to go to the
emperor. But his parents advised him to be brave and honest. "At least you worked
hard and tried. That is what matters." He decided to go back and return the royal
seed. When he reached the Royal court, he was
disheartened to see that every
other child had a beautiful flowering plant in his pot. He quietly took his place in
the queue. Soon, the emperor came and started inspecting every pot. At last he
came up to Ping. "Why is your pot empty? Did you not look after the royal seed
properly?" , he asked.
"I did, Your Majesty." , replied Ping timidly. "I changed the soil, watered it
regularly, put it out in the sun and air but nothing happened. I am sorry to
disappoint you."
The emperor smiled and clapped him on the back. "At last I have found an honest
boy worthy of becoming a king. All the royal seeds were boiled. And as we all know,
boiled seeds don't grow into plants. Shame on all you dishonest parents who
taught their children to lie."
The wise indeed say:
Honesty is the best policy
