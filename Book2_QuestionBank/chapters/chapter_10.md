The Banyan Tree
The Banyan Tree can be seen in many places in India. It is a huge tree. It has
aerial roots which grow from the branches and descend to the ground. And
then from these roots the tree spreads further. This tree has a very long
life-span. The average life span of a banyan tree is 200-500 years. This tree
is very useful. In Ayurveda, its fruit is used to treat inflammation as well as
skin irritation. Paper is also made from the wood of this tree.
This tree is considered sacred in religions like Hinduism and Buddhism.
The Banyan Tree is the national tree of India.
