**Question**:
Higher Order Helping Verb Reasoning:

Correct the sentence: 'Here **is** the paper-thin chips you asked for.'

- (A) Here **are** the paper-thin chips you asked for.
- (B) Here am the paper-thin chips you asked for.
- (C) Here be the paper-thin chips you asked for.

**Question**:
Higher Order Helping Verb Reasoning:

Identify the sentence where 'is' acts as a MAIN linking verb rather than a helping verb:

- (A) George Crum **is** an innovative chef.
- (B) George Crum **is** slicing the potatoes.
- (C) George Crum **is** frying the chips.
- (D) George Crum **is** serving the guests.

- **Answer Key**: **(A) George Crum **is** an innovative chef.** — In 'George Crum is an innovative chef', 'is' is the main linking verb connecting subject to predicate noun.

Higher Order Helping Verb Reasoning:

Select the option with PERFECT helping verb agreement throughout:

- (A) The chef is talented, I am hungry, and the chips are salty.
- (B) The chef are talented, I is hungry, and the chips is salty.
