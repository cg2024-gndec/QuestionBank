- (B) The chef fried an potatoes in a hot oil.
- (C) An chef fried the potatoes in the hot oil.
- (D) A chef fried a potatoes in a hot oil.

- **Answer Key**: **(A) A chef fried the potatoes in hot oil.** — 'A chef' (/sh/ sound), 'the potatoes' (plural), 'hot oil' (mass noun, no 'a').
