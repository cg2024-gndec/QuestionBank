**Question**:
Higher Order Action Verb Analysis:

Which sentence uses action verbs to show cause and effect?

- (A) She **wrote** beautiful plays, so her parents **sent** her to England.
- (B) Sarojini Naidu was a poet and her father was an educationist.
- (C) Her voice was soft and gentle.
- (D) Hyderabad is in British India.

- **Answer Key**: **(A) She **wrote** beautiful plays, so her parents **sent** her to England.** — 'wrote' (cause action) -> 'sent' (effect action).
