Correct the sentence: 'An hippopotamus never rides in a omnibus.'

- (B) The hippopotamus never rides in an omnibus.

**Question**:
Higher Order Article Reasoning:

Identify the correct rule for using 'the' with animal species in literary/poetic subjects (the lion, the elephant, the hippopotamus):

- (A) The definite article 'the' is used with a singular countable noun to represent a whole class or species.

- **Answer Key**: **(A) The definite article 'the' is used with a singular countable noun to represent a whole class or species.** — Definite article represents an entire species.
