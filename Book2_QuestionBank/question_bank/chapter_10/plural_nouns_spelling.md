Fill in the blanks: 'The two ____ (child) climbed two ____ (branch) of the tree.'

- (A) children, branches
- (B) childs, branchs
- (C) childrens, branchies
- (D) childes, branches

- **Answer Key**: **(A) children, branches** — child -> children; branch -> branches (-ch + es).
