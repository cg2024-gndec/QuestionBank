**Question**:
Calendar Logic & Mathematical Time Reasoning:

Which month pair both have 31 days and come right after each other during summer monsoon?

- (A) July and August
- (B) June and July
- (C) May and June
- (D) August and September

- **Answer Key**: **(A) July and August** — July (31) and August (31) are consecutive 31-day months.
