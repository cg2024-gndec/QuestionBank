| **CH08-GRA-004** | **(A)** | Passive Voice: Swadeshi movement (Object) + was supported by + Bhagat Singh. |
| **CH08-GRA-005** | **(B)** | 'Brilliant' describes boy (Adjective). |
| **CH08-GRA-006** | **(A)** | Young -> Younger -> Youngest. |
| **CH08-GRA-007** | **(B)** | Deep -> Deeper -> Deepest. |
| **CH08-GRA-008** | **(A)** | Past tense of bring is brought. |
| **CH08-GRA-009** | **(A)** | Past tense of wear is wore. |
| **CH08-GRA-010** | **(B)** | 'Himself' is an Emphatic/Reflexive Pronoun. |
| **CH08-GRA-011** | **(B)** | Preposition 'on' before specific calendar date (on September 28, 1907). |
| **CH08-GRA-012** | **(B)** | Preposition 'of' after full (full of mud). |
| **CH08-GRA-013** | **(A)** | Conjunction 'and' joins two positive daily routine actions. |
| **CH08-GRA-014** | **(A)** | Conjunction 'so' shows cause and effect. |
| **CH08-GRA-015** | **(B)** | 'Extensively' modifies wrote (Adverb of manner/degree). |
| **CH08-GRA-016** | **(A)** | Indefinite article 'a' before consonant sound 'brilliant'. |
| **CH08-GRA-017** | **(C)** | Definite article 'the' before specific noun 'the land of Jallianwala Bagh'. |
| **CH08-GRA-018** | **(B)** | Plural subject 'The three revolutionaries' takes plural past verb 'were'. |
| **CH08-GRA-019** | **(A)** | Statement of fact (Declarative). |
| **CH08-GRA-020** | **(B)** | Question ending with question mark (Interrogative). |
| **CH08-GRA-021** | **(B)** | Hero + es = Heroes. |
| **CH08-GRA-022** | **(B)** | Noun ending in -y changes to -ies: Atrocities. |
| **CH08-GRA-023** | **(A)** | 'Not a coward' preserves the meaning of brave. |
| **CH08-GRA-024** | **(B)** | Capitalization of date, names, and commas are correct. |
| **CH08-GRA-025** | **(B)** | Singular possessive: Bhagat Singh's sacrifice. |
