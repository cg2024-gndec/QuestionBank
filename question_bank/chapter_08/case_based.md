## Scenario 1: Standing Against Injustice
> Karan witnessed an older student bullying younger children on the school playground. Instead of ignoring the situation, Karan politely but firmly reported the issue to the teacher, standing up for his classmates' safety.

  - (C) Mindless obedience to bullies
  - (D) Running away from problems
