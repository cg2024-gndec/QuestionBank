> **Reading Context**:
> Passage 1: (From 'Empty Pot')
> Once upon a time in China, there lived a boy named Ping. He loved plants and gardening. The Emperor of the land had no children of his own. As he had grown very old, he was worried about the fate of the kingdom. He wanted to find a worthy successor of the crown. He announced that he would give a task to the children and the winner would be nominated the next king.

- **Question**: Where did Ping live?
