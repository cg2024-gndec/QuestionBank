### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH01-GL-001
- **Type**: GL
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Apply
- **Topic**: Degrees of Comparison
- **Marks**: 2
- **Question**: Fill in the table with the correct degrees of comparison:

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| brave | ___________ | ___________ |

- **Answer Key**: brave → **braver** → **bravest**
