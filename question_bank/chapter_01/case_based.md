# Case & Scenario Based Questions - Chapter 01: Empty Pot

---

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH01-CAS-001
- **Type**: Case_Based
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Bloom Level**: Apply
- **Topic**: Honesty in Real Life
- **Marks**: 3
- **Case / Scenario**: Scenario: Rohan breaks a glass vase while playing indoor cricket. His mother asks who broke it. Rohan admits his mistake immediately. How is Rohan's behavior similar to Ping's in 'Empty Pot'?
- **Model Answer**: Rohan shows honesty and courage by telling the truth despite fear of punishment, just like Ping brought an empty pot and confessed the truth to the Emperor.

---

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: CH01-CAS-002
- **Type**: Case_Based
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Bloom Level**: Evaluate
- **Topic**: Academic Integrity
- **Marks**: 3
- **Case / Scenario**: Scenario: Priya's science experiment fails to produce crystals, while her classmate buys readymade crystals to get full marks. If you were Priya, what would you do and why?
- **Model Answer**: I would submit my original experiment with an honest report explaining what went wrong, rather than cheating. Like Ping, being truthful proves genuine effort and integrity.

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: CH01-CAS-003
- **Type**: Case_Based
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Bloom Level**: Create
- **Topic**: Test Design
- **Marks**: 3
- **Case / Scenario**: Scenario: An ancient ruler wants to test which advisor is most trustworthy. Inspired by the story 'Empty Pot', what test could the ruler design?
