# Long Answer & HOTS Questions - Chapter 01: Empty Pot

---

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH01-LON-001
- **Type**: Long Answer / HOTS
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Bloom Level**: Analyze
- **Topic**: Plot & Summary
- **Marks**: 5
- **Question**: Describe the Emperor's test to find a successor. How did Ping participate in this test?
- **Model Answer**:
**The Emperor's Test**: The Emperor of China was getting old and had no heir. To select a worthy successor, he announced a contest giving every child a seed. They were instructed to grow it for six months and present their plants at the court.
**Ping's Participation**: Ping loved gardening and cared for his seed deeply by giving it fresh soil, water, and sunlight. However, nothing grew because the seed was boiled. While other children cheated by substituting fake flowers, Ping obeyed his parents and brought his empty pot truthfully. The Emperor rewarded Ping's honesty by crowning him king.

- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Bloom Level**: Evaluate
- **Topic**: Character Comparison
- **Marks**: 5
- **Question**: Why do you think the other children replaced their seeds, while Ping brought an empty pot? Compare their values.
- **Model Answer**:
**The Other Children**: Driven by greed and fear of failure, the other children substituted the boiled seeds with fake blooming plants. They prioritized winning over truthfulness.
**Ping's Values**: Ping cared about integrity. When his hard work yielded no plant, he was brave enough to present his honest effort. Supported by his parents, Ping chose truth over deceit.
**Conclusion**: The story highlights that genuine character shines in honesty, whereas deceitful shortcuts ultimately lead to failure.

### Question 15
- **Question ID**: CH01-LON-015
- **Type**: Long Answer / HOTS
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Bloom Level**: Apply
- **Topic**: Science Integration
- **Marks**: 5
- **Question**: What scientific principle explains why Ping's seed did not grow?
- **Model Answer**:
The scientific principle is that extreme heat (boiling) denatures the proteins and kills the cellular embryo inside a plant seed. Once a seed is boiled, its biological life is destroyed, making germination impossible regardless of soil, water, or sunlight.
