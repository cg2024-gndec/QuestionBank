## Passage Set 1 (Q1-Q4)

**Read the passage/poem extract and answer the questions:**

I'm shouting / I'm singing / I'm swinging through trees / I'm winging sky high / With the buzzing black bees. / I'm the sun / I'm the moon / I'm the dew on the rose. / I'm a rabbit / Whose habit / Is twitching his nose.

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH13-RC-001
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Action
- **Question**: What is the speaker doing in the first stanza?
    - (A) Sleeping and dreaming
    - (B) Shouting, singing, and swinging through trees
    - (C) Crying quietly
    - (D) Reading a book
- **Answer Key**: (B) Shouting, singing, and swinging through trees

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: CH13-RC-002
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Animal
- **Question**: Which animal is mentioned with a habit of twitching its nose?
    - (A) A dog
    - (B) A rabbit
    - (C) A cat
    - (D) A lamb
- **Answer Key**: (B) A rabbit

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: CH13-RC-003
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Poem Detail
- **Question**: What is the dew on?
    - (A) The grass
    - (B) The rose
    - (C) The tree
    - (D) The roof
- **Answer Key**: (B) The rose

### Question 4
- **Question ID**: CH13-RC-004
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Rhyming Words
- **Question**: Find a rhyming pair from the lines:

- **Answer Key**: (A) trees - bees
