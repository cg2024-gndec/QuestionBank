## Passage Set 1 (Q1-Q4)

**Read the passage/poem extract and answer the questions:**

The river Kaveri, sometimes spelt as Cauvery, is one of the major rivers in India. It originates in Brahmagiri Hill in the Western Ghats of South Karnataka. The source of the river is called Talakaveri. Flowing from west to east, the river splits into many tributaries and falls into the Bay of Bengal.

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH12-RC-001
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Origin
- **Question**: Where does the Kaveri river originate?

    - (B) Brahmagiri Hill
    - (C) Aravalli Range
    - (D) Satpura Range
- **Answer Key**: (B) Brahmagiri Hill

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: CH12-RC-002
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Destination
- **Question**: Into which body of water does the Kaveri flow?
    - (A) Arabian Sea
    - (B) Indian Ocean
    - (C) Bay of Bengal
    - (D) Red Sea
- **Answer Key**: (C) Bay of Bengal

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: CH12-RC-003
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Source Name
- **Question**: What is the source of the river called?
    - (A) Brahmagiri
    - (B) Talakaveri
    - (C) Srirangam
    - (D) Shivasamudram
- **Answer Key**: (B) Talakaveri

### Question 4

- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 2
