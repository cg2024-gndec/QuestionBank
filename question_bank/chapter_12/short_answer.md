### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH12-SA-001
- **Type**: SA
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Origin
- **Question**: Where does the Kaveri river originate?
- **Answer Key**: The Kaveri river originates in **Brahmagiri Hill** in the Western Ghats of South Karnataka.
