## Passage Set 1 (Q1-Q4)

**Read the passage/poem extract and answer the questions:**

They say that God lives very high! / But if you look above the pines / You cannot see our God. And why? / And if you dig down in the mines / You never see Him in the gold, / Though from Him all that's glory shines.

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH15-RC-001
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Poem Detail
- **Question**: Where do people say God lives?
    - (A) In the ocean
    - (B) Very high above
    - (C) In the mines
    - (D) Under trees
- **Answer Key**: (B) Very high above

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: CH15-RC-002
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Poem Detail
- **Question**: Can we physically see God above the pine trees or in gold mines?
    - (A) Yes, easily
    - (B) No, you cannot see Him physically
    - (C) Only at night
    - (D) Only in winter
- **Answer Key**: (B) No, you cannot see Him physically

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: CH15-RC-003
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Poem Detail
- **Question**: Whose glory shines in all things according to the poet?
    - (A) The king's
    - (B) God's
    - (C) The sun's
    - (D) The wind's
- **Answer Key**: (B) God's

### Question 4
- **Question ID**: CH15-RC-004
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Rhyming Words
- **Question**: Find a rhyming pair from these lines:
    - (A) high - why
    - (B) pines - gold
    - (C) God - shines
    - (D) see - lives
- **Answer Key**: (A) high - why
