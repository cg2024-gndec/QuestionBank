- **Question**: Who wrote the poem 'A Child's Thought of God'?

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: CH15-SA-002
- **Type**: SA
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Poem Meaning
- **Question**: Why can't we physically see God above the pines or in gold mines?
- **Answer Key**: Because God's presence is spiritual and everywhere in creation, not a physical form you can see with human eyes.

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: CH15-SA-003
- **Type**: SA
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Marks**: 2
- **Topic**: Simile
- **Question**: What does the poet compare God's gentle embrace to?
- **Answer Key**: The poet compares God's embrace to a **tender mother's kiss on her child's shut eyelids at night**.
