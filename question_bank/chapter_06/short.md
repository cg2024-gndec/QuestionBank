## Answer Key & Model Answers

| Question ID | Model Answer (Class IV Standard) |
|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| **CH06-SA-001** | Lata Mangeshkar was born on 28th September 1929 in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. |
| **CH06-SA-002** | Her parents were Pandit Deenanath Mangeshkar and Shevanti. Her father was a classical singer and ran a theatre company. |
| **CH06-SA-003** | Her father was her first music teacher. She started acting in musical plays at the young age of five. |
| **CH06-SA-004** | Her father passed away when she was only 13 years old. She had to sing and act to earn money and support her mother and four younger siblings. |
| **CH06-SA-005** | She recorded her first song in 1942 and her first Hindi song in 1943. |
| **CH06-SA-006** | Her career spanned over seven decades (70+ years), during which she sang songs in numerous Indian languages. |
| **CH06-SA-007** | She sang more than 50,000 songs and holds the Guinness World Record for singing the most songs in the world. |
| **CH06-SA-008** | She received the Bharat Ratna (highest civilian award) in 2001 and was honored with the title *Swar Kokila* of India. |
| **CH06-SA-009** | She passed away on 6th February 2022. Her memory remains because her immortal songs will be loved for eternity. |
| **CH06-SA-010** | 'Iconic' means being a symbolic representative of an art form; 'revered' means being greatly respected and honored by millions. |
| **CH06-SA-011** | A breadwinner is the primary earner for a family's basic needs. At age 13, Lata sang in films to provide food and shelter for her family. |
| **CH06-SA-012** | 'Unprecedented' means never seen before; 'eternity' means endless time. |
| **CH06-SA-013** | She remained deeply humble, simple, respectful, and completely devoted to music throughout her lifetime. |
| **CH06-SA-014** | *Her first Hindi song was recorded by Lata Mangeshkar in 1943.* |
| **CH06-SA-015** | *Lata Mangeshkar inspired millions of aspiring singers.* |
| **CH06-SA-016** | **Proper Nouns**: Pandit Deenanath, Indore.<br>**Common Nouns**: music, daughter. |
| **CH06-SA-017** | 'Swar' means musical note/melody and 'Kokila' means cuckoo bird. The title was given for her sweet, flawless, and melodious voice. |
| **CH06-SA-018** | Losing her father early taught her responsibility, discipline, and tireless effort, shaping her into a dedicated legend. |
| **CH06-SA-019** | (a) **Humble**: Proud / Arrogant  
(b) **Revered**: Disrespected / Despised |
| **CH06-SA-020** | *In 2001, Lata Mangeshkar received the Bharat Ratna in New Delhi.* |
| **CH06-SA-021** | For seven decades, her voice defined playback singing, giving life to legendary film songs across generations. |
| **CH06-SA-022** | A playback singer pre-records songs that actors lip-sync on screen. Lata recorded over 50,000 playback tracks for Indian cinema. |
| **CH06-SA-023** | Singing in many regional languages allowed people across India to connect with her voice, uniting diverse cultures through music. |
| **CH06-SA-024** | (a) High, **Higher**, Highest  
(b) Exceptional, **More Exceptional**, Most Exceptional |
| **CH06-SA-025** | Born in 1929, Lata Mangeshkar overcame early hardship to become India's legendary playback singer, recording over 50,000 songs. Awarded the Bharat Ratna and titled Swar Kokila, her musical legacy lives on forever. |
