## Extract 1
> *"Lata Mangeshkar is one of the most iconic and revered playback singers in the history of Indian music. She was born on 28th September 1929 to Pandit Deenanath Mangeshkar and Shevanti in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Her father was a classical singer and ran a theatre company that produced musical plays. Lata Mangeshkar showed a deep interest in music from an early age and her father became her first teacher. She also started acting at the age of five. But she lost her father at the tender age of 13 and became the family's sole breadwinner. She struggled to establish herself as a singer to look after her mother and four siblings."*
