### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH02-GW-001
- **Type**: GW
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 6
- **Topic**: About the Jackal
- **Question**: Complete the paragraph using the sentence frames:
  - The jackal in the story was very ___.
  - He lived in a ___.
  - One day, he found ___ near his cave.
  - He was clever because ___.
  - The jackal decided to ___.
  - His presence of mind saved ___.
- **Model Answer**:
  - The jackal in the story was very clever.
  - He lived in a cave in the jungle.
  - One day, he found lion footprints near his cave.
  - He was clever because he thought before acting.
  - The jackal decided to shout at the cave to test it.
  - His presence of mind saved his life.
