| **CH02-SA-001** | Kharanakhara was a lion. He lived in and ruled over a certain part of a jungle. |

| **CH02-SA-003** | He thought that some animal must live in such a nice cave, and he decided to hide inside to eat whichever animal returned at sunset. |
| **CH02-SA-004** | A jackal lived in the big cave. Upon returning, he noticed lion footprints leading into the cave, but no footprints coming out. |
| **CH02-SA-005** | He noticed footprints of a lion going inside his cave, but could not see any footprints coming back out, which alerted him to the danger inside. |
| **CH02-SA-006** | He called out to the cave, pretending it always replied before he entered. He threatened to go to another cave if it stayed silent. |
| **CH02-SA-007** | The lion feared that if the cave remained silent, the jackal would suspect danger and go to another cave for shelter. |
| **CH02-SA-008** | The lion said: *"Hello Jackal, you may enter. It is safe inside."* |
| **CH02-SA-009** | It was frightening because the sound echoed off the cave walls, making the roar sound very loud and booming. |
| **CH02-SA-010** | The jackal realized the lion was inside waiting to eat him, so he ran off as fast as he could to save his life. |
| **CH02-SA-011** | We learn that presence of mind and quick thinking can save us from dangerous situations. |
| **CH02-SA-012** | 'Uninhabited' means having nobody living in it. *Sentence: The old house on the hill has been uninhabited for years.* |
| **CH02-SA-013** | 'Presence of mind' means the ability to stay calm, observe carefully, and think quickly during a sudden danger or emergency. |
| **CH02-SA-014** | The lion had great physical strength but lacked cleverness. The jackal was physically small but saved his life using mental sharpness and wit. |
| **CH02-SA-015** | *The jackal runs off as fast as he can.* |
| **CH02-SA-016** | He claimed that the cave always answered his greeting before he entered, and that he only entered after getting permission. |
| **CH02-SA-017** | The lion believed that caves can talk and answered on behalf of the cave, thereby exposing his own presence inside. |
| **CH02-SA-018** | Panchatantra is an ancient collection of Indian animal fables designed to teach moral values, practical wisdom, and governance. |
| **CH02-SA-019** | Sunset was the time when wild animals returned to their shelters and when hungry predators looked for prey. |
| **CH02-SA-020** | (a) **Inhabited** / Populated  
(b) **Pleasant** / Comforting / Calming |
| **CH02-SA-021** | He was cautious because he observed lion footprints leading in, and he knew entering blindly could cost him his life. |
| **CH02-SA-022** | *"Hello Cave! Can I come inside?" asked the jackal.* |
| **CH02-SA-023** | If he had ignored the footprints, he would have entered the cave unwarned and been killed and eaten by the lion. |
| **CH02-SA-024** | An echo is a sound repeated because of sound waves bouncing back from walls. It made the lion's deep voice sound louder and scarier. |
| **CH02-SA-025** | *"Presence of mind can save you from danger."* |
