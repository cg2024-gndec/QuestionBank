On his way, he saw a big cave, and thought to himself, 'It is for sure that some animal must be living in this big cave. Such a nice cave cannot be uninhabited. ## Extract 4
> *"On hearing this, the lion thought, 'The cave must reply to the jackal when he returns at sunset. It is because I am inside that the cave is not replying today. I must invite on behalf of the cave, or the jackal will go away.' So, the lion replied from inside the cave, 'Hello Jackal, you may enter. It is safe inside.' The reply was even more frightening due to the echo from the walls of the cave. The jackal at once knew that the lion was waiting for him to enter and make a meal out of him. He ran off, as fast as he could and saved himself. The wise indeed say: Presence of mind can save you from danger."*
