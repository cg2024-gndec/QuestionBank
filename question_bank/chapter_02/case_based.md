## Scenario 1: Rohan and the Locked Room
> Rohan reached home from school and noticed that the main front door was slightly open. His parents were usually at work at this time. He saw unfamiliar muddy boot prints leading inside the living room, but no footprints coming back out toward the gate.

  - (D) Both returned home at sunrise.
