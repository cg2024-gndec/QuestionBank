## Extract 1
> *"While moving on road we all come across the traffic signal, which turns from red to yellow to green at regular intervals. And what is the purpose of this colourful display? It is to regulate the flow of automobiles and help the pedestrians at the crossroads. This is done to reduce the chances of accidents on roads. But interestingly the traffic signals came into existence long before automobiles were invented. The idea for developing traffic signals began in the 1800s when people either walked or were driven around on horse-drawn carriages."*

## Extract 3
> *"Red light signaled carriages to stop as red represented caution or danger. Green meant to proceed as green was determined to be a more reassuring colour in most cultures. This system had so many merits that soon it was implemented and readily followed all over the country. Soon this system was adopted all over the world. With time the yellow colour was added to give time to commuters to get ready to move. Earlier the lights were gas-fuelled and manually controlled and it became a very tricky and strenuous job. But now we have advanced electronic automated lights at all the important places all over the world. These indeed help the traffic to move smoothly and reach their destination timely."*
