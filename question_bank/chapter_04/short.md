| **CH04-SA-004** | Electric traffic lights were invented by Garrett Morgan in the year 1923. |

| **CH04-SA-007** | Yellow was added later to give commuters time to prepare and get ready to move before the light turned green or red. |
| **CH04-SA-008** | Earlier traffic lights were gas-fuelled and manually operated, whereas modern lights are electronic, automated, and computer-controlled. |
| **CH04-SA-009** | The first traffic lights in 1868 controlled horse-drawn carriage traffic and helped walking pedestrians cross the street. |
| **CH04-SA-010** | A pedestrian is a person walking on the street. Traffic lights give pedestrians dedicated green signals to cross roads safely without fear of oncoming traffic. |
| **CH04-SA-011** | A commuter is a person who travels regularly between work and home. *Example: An office worker taking the city bus daily is a commuter.* |
| **CH04-SA-012** | Crossroads is an intersection where two or more roads cross each other. Without signals, vehicles coming from different directions would collide. |

| **CH04-SA-014** | *Electric traffic lights regulated road traffic smoothly.* |
