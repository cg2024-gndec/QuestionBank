### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH03-GW-001
- **Type**: GW
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 6
- **Topic**: About The
- **Question**: Complete the paragraph using the sentence frames:
  - My name is ___.
  - I am ___ years old.
  - I read in ___.
  - I love ___.
  - My favourite thing is ___.
  - I believe that ___.
- **Model Answer**:
  - My name is Aryan.
  - I am nine years old.
  - I read in Class Four.
  - I love reading and sports.
  - My favourite thing is spending time with family.
  - I believe every day brings new learning.
