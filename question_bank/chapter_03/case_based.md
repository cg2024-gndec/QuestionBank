## Scenario 1: The Mosquito Problem
> Sameer was studying for his annual exams when a mosquito began buzzing around his little brother's face while he was taking an afternoon nap. Sameer got angry at the mosquito, picked up a heavy wooden cricket bat, and swung it at his brother's forehead to hit the mosquito.

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH03-CAS-001
- **Type**: Case Based
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Bloom Level**: Apply
- **Topic**: Danger Analogy
- **Marks**: 1
- **Question**: How does Sameer's action mirror the foolish monkey's action in Chapter 03?
  - (A) Both used gentle hand fans.
  - (B) Both used a heavy/dangerous instrument to swat a tiny insect sitting on a sleeping person's body.
  - (C) Both called a doctor.
  - (D) Both fell asleep.

### Question 2
