## Answer Key

| Question ID | Correct Option | Explanation |
|-------------|:--------------:|-------------|
| **CH03-GRA-001** | **(B)** | 'King' is a Common Noun referring to a ruler. |
| **CH03-GRA-002** | **(B)** | A group of monkeys is called a 'troop'. |
| **CH03-GRA-003** | **(C)** | 'Foolish' describes the monkey (Adjective). |
| **CH03-GRA-004** | **(C)** | Intelligent -> More intelligent -> Most intelligent. |
| **CH03-GRA-005** | **(D)** | Foolish -> More foolish -> Most foolish. |
| **CH03-GRA-006** | **(B)** | The past tense of strike is struck. |
| **CH03-GRA-007** | **(B)** | The past tense of fly is flew. |
| **CH03-GRA-008** | **(B)** | 'He' replaces the King (Pronoun). |
| **CH03-GRA-009** | **(C)** | 'Peacefully' modifies sleeping (Adverb of manner). |
| **CH03-GRA-010** | **(B)** | 'Severely' modifies wounded (Adverb of manner). |
| **CH03-GRA-011** | **(A)** | Preposition 'on' indicates surface contact. |
| **CH03-GRA-012** | **(A)** | Preposition 'into' indicates entry inside. |
| **CH03-GRA-013** | **(A)** | 'But' shows contrast between flicking and returning. |
| **CH03-GRA-014** | **(B)** | 'But / while' shows contrasting outcomes. |
| **CH03-GRA-015** | **(B)** | Said that the monkey was foolish (past reporting verb). |
| **CH03-GRA-016** | **(A)** | Indefinite article 'a' before consonant sound 'fly'. |
| **CH03-GRA-017** | **(C)** | Definite article 'the' before specific time 'afternoon'. |
| **CH03-GRA-018** | **(B)** | Plural subject 'Servants' takes plural verb 'were'. |
| **CH03-GRA-019** | **(B)** | Gives a command/warning (Imperative). |
| **CH03-GRA-020** | **(B)** | Asks a question with a question mark (Interrogative). |
| **CH03-GRA-021** | **(B)** | Nouns ending in 'y' after a consonant change to 'ies' (flies). |
| **CH03-GRA-022** | **(B)** | Nouns ending in 'y' after a vowel add 's' (monkeys). |
| **CH03-GRA-023** | **(A)** | 'Not wise at all' preserves the meaning of being foolish. |
| **CH03-GRA-024** | **(B)** | Singular possessive uses apostrophe-s (king's). |
| **CH03-GRA-025** | **(A)** | Singular possessive: 'king's personal chambers'. |
