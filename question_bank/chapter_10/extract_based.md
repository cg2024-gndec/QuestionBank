## Extract 1
> *"The Kerala boat race has a deep cultural and historical significance in the southern state of India. It is one of the most thrilling and iconic traditions of 'God's own land'. This tradition dates back several centuries with its origins often linked to the region's ancient maritime heritage. These races were initially part of religious maritime festivals where they were held to seek blessings for prosperity and good harvests. The boat race is known as 'Vallam Kali' translates to 'boat game' and it involves long, sleek boats called chundan vallams or snake boats."*

## Extract 2
> *"Each boat is a marvel of craftsmanship. It is traditionally built from a single tree. The rowers wear uniforms and chant Vanchipattu (boat songs) in sync with their strokes and perform as a team. Over time, the Kerala boat race has evolved into a major event that draws large crowds of locals as well as foreigners. It is not only a thrilling sport but also a colourful cultural display of Kerala's unique traditions, music and dance, which has made it an integral part of the state's identity and tourism."*
