# Guided Writing -- Chapter 14: Be Kind

> **Format**: 6-sentence guided paragraph/description frames. 50 tasks.

---

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH14-GW-001
- **Type**: GW
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 6
- **Topic**: Acts of Kindness
- **Question**: Complete the paragraph using the sentence frames:
  - Kindness means helping ___.
  - I can show kindness by ___.
  - When I speak softly, people feel ___.
  - Helping an old person brings ___.
  - God loves people who are ___.
  - A little kindness goes a long ___.
- **Model Answer**:
  - Kindness means helping others with a gentle heart.
  - I can show kindness by sharing my things and speaking politely.
  - When I speak softly, people feel respected and happy.
  - Helping an old person brings a smile to their face.
  - God loves people who are kind and forgiving.
  - A little kindness goes a long, long way.
