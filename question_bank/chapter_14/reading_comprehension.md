## Passage Set 1 (Q1-Q4)

**Read the passage/poem extract and answer the questions:**

Just a little bit of kindness / Can go a long, long way, / Just a little bit of tenderness / Can brighten up a day. / Just a bit of praise where it's deserved / Can bring a happy glow, / Just a hand held out can give some hope / To someone feeling low.
