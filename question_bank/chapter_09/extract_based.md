## Extract 1
> *"Garba is a lively and colourful folk dance from the state of Gujarat. It has been in practice for centuries and its roots can be traced back to ancient Gujarat. It is a perfect blend of devotion and culture as it combines elements of dance and prayers. The word Garba is derived from the Sanskrit word 'Garbha' meaning 'womb', and symbolizes life and creation. According to popular belief, in its initial days, the Garba folk dance was performed as an act to pay homage to Goddess Durga, who is worshipped through the Navratri festival."*

## Extract 2
> *"This dance is performed around an earthen pot with holes or 'garbo', filled with water and decked with lamps, and people used to dance around it while singing devotional songs in praise of the goddess. The Garba has become inherent to the festival of Navratri, consisting of nine nights in honour and worship of Goddess Durga. Women wear colourful and embroidered chaniya choli adorned with traditional jewellery. Men participate wearing kediyu (short frock-style tops) and dhotis."*
