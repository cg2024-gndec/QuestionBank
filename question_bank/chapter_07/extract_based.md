> *"Ramakant Achrekar spotted his exceptional talent and coached him initially. But Tendulkar still consulted him even after becoming an icon. This shows his humble nature and simplicity. He has received Arjun Award, Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna Award and Padma Vibhushan and many other awards. His crowning glory came in 2014, when he became the youngest and the first sports person ever to receive the Bharat Ratna, India's highest Civilian honour.
