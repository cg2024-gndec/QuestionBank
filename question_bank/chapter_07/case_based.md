## Scenario 1: Dealing with Lack of Luxuries
> Rohan, a 10-year-old student, complained to his parents that he could not practice tennis because he owned only one pair of sports shoes, whereas his classmates had three pairs. His father shared Sachin Tendulkar's childhood story with him.

  - (C) By telling him to quit tennis.
  - (D) By buying ten pairs of shoes.
