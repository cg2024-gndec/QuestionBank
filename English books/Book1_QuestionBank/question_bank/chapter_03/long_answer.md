**Question**: Write a simple summary of the story 'The Elephants and the Mice'.

- **Answer Key**: An earthquake destroyed a village, and a colony of mice made it their home. A herd of giant elephants passed through to reach a lake, accidentally trampling many mice. The King of Mice requested the Elephant King to change their route, promising to return the favor. The Elephant King laughed but agreed. Later, hunters trapped the elephants in rope nets. The mice came immediately, chewed through the ropes with their sharp teeth, and freed the elephants. The Elephant King thanked the mice warmly.
