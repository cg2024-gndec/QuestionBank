**Question**: Write a simple summary of Chapter 05 'Father of the Nation'.

- **Answer Key**: Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was born in Porbandar, Gujarat on October 2, 1869. He studied law in London and became a Barrister. He practiced law at the Bombay High Court and later moved to South Africa, where he started Satyagraha—a non-violent protest against oppression. Returning to India, he led the freedom struggle against British rule using non-violence, truth, and Swadeshi goods. He lived a simple life, spun thread on the Charkha, and endured jail for India's freedom. He is loved by all as the Father of the Nation, and his birthday is celebrated as Gandhi Jayanti.
