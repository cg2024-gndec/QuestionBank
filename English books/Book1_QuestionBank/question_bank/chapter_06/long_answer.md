**Question**: Write a simple summary of Chapter 06 'My Favourite Cartoon'.

- **Answer Key**: Chhota Bheem is a popular cartoon about a 9-year-old boy living in Dholakpur village. Bheem is intelligent, brave, and strong. He loves eating delicious laddoos made by Tuntun Mausi, which give him extra strength. Bheem always protects the villagers and is loved by everyone. Kaliya is a boy who gets jealous of Bheem's popularity and tries to outdo him with his twin sidekicks Dholu and Bholu, but fails every time. Bheem's good friends are Chhutki, Raju, and Jaggu the monkey. Bheem is always kind, helpful, and humble.
