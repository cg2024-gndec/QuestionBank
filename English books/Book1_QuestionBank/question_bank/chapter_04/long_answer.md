**Question**: Write a simple summary of the story of 'The Popsicle'.

- **Answer Key**: In 1905, an 11-year-old boy named Frank Epperson in San Francisco mixed sugary soda powder with water in a cup and left a wooden stirrer stick inside. He accidentally left the cup outside on a freezing cold night. By morning, the liquid had frozen solid around the stick. Frank licked the icy treat off the stick and loved it. He named it 'Epsicle' and sold it to neighbors. Years later, his children renamed it 'Popsicle' to honor their father.
