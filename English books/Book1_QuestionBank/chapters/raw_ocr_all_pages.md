the most popular historical figure
in India. He was born in Porbandar,
Gujarat, on October 2, 1869. His family

violent protest against the oppression of Europeans. He soon returned to
India and joined the struggle for independence from British rule.
He was a simple man who taught simplicity and self-reliance to the people of
India. He encouraged them to boycott foreign goods and make their
Swadeshi goods. He was loved and respected by people of all communities.
MS a freedom fighter, Gandhi was a man of firm belief. During his freedom
struggle, the British had put him in jail several times, where he endured
extreme hardships. To honour his role in the freedom struggle, his birthday
is celebrated as a national festival to pay homage to him and everyone who
stood with him.
