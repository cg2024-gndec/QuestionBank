# 🧠 Book 1 — Class I English Question Bank Intelligence & Taxonomy

> **Class**: I | **Board**: CBSE / ICSE | **Subject**: English (Language & Literature)  
> **Source Text**: *My Book of English COMPREHENSION & COMPOSITION 1* (`COMPREHENSION BOOK—1.pdf`)  
> **Chapters**: 15 | **Questions per Chapter**: 300 (6 categories × 50)

---

## Project Objective

Create a high-quality, exam-aligned Question Bank for Class I English that:
- Mirrors exact CBSE/ICSE Class I English question types (adapted for early learners)
- Covers every chapter's story, poem, vocabulary, and composition themes from `COMPREHENSION BOOK—1.pdf`
- Maintains clear, age-appropriate language for students aged 5–7 years

---

## 6-Category Question Taxonomy

| # | Category | File | ID Prefix | Marks | Description |
|---|----------|------|-----------|:-----:|-------------|
| 1 | **MCQs** | `mcqs.md` | `BK01_CHXX_MCQ_NNN` | 1 | 4-option MCQs: story recall, character identification, picture/word facts |
| 2 | **Fill in the Blanks** | `fill_in_the_blanks.md` | `BK01_CHXX_FIB_NNN` | 1 | General sentence completion: sight words, basic grammar, key concepts |
| 3 | **Fill in Blanks from Story** | `fill_in_blanks_story.md` | `BK01_CHXX_STORY_FIB_NNN` | 1 | Story Cloze passage fillups directly from chapter text |
| 4 | **True / False** | `true_false.md` | `BK01_CHXX_TF_NNN` | 1 | Story-based statement validation with clear reasons |
| 5 | **Short Answer** | `short_answer.md` | `BK01_CHXX_SA_NNN` | 2 | Concise 1–2 sentence responses: character names, settings, moral recall |
| 6 | **Long Answer** | `long_answer.md` | `BK01_CHXX_LA_NNN` | 5 | 3–5 sentence guided responses: story summary, character analysis, moral discussion |
