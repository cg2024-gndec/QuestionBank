Kaveri
The river Kaveri, sometimes spelt as Cauvery, is one of the major rivers in India.
It originates in Brahmagiri Hill in the Western Ghats of South Karnataka. The
source of the river is called Talakaveri. Flowing from west to east, the river splits
into many tributaries and falls into the Bay of Bengal. Before merging with the
bay, it forms a fertile delta called the 'garden of southern Indial. It has the total
length of around 800 kilometres and covers Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and
Puducherry in its journey.
The Kaveri river is revered in Hindu mythology and is associated with several
legends. Many people believe that bathing in its waters can cleanse sins and bring
spiritual satisfaction. Srirangapatna and Srirangam are famous pilgrimage towns
situated on its banks.
The Kaveri helps in irrigation as well as is used to generate hydroelectric power.
The Shivasamudram falls supply hydroelectricity to Mysore, Bengaluru and Kolar
districts. Its most fertile area is its delta in Tamil Nadu. This delta is known as
the "Granary of South India"as it supports extensive agriculture specially of rice
and sugarcane.
