Lata Mangeshkar is one of the most
iconic and

playback singers in the history of
Indian music. She was born on 28th September
1929 to Pandit Deenanath Mangeshkar and
Shevanti in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Her
father was a classical singer and ran a theatre
company that produced musical plays. Lata
Mangeshkar showed a deep interest in music
from an early age and her father became
her first teacher. She also started acting
at the age of five. But she lost her father

at the tender age of 13 and became the family's sole breadwinner. She struggled to
establish herself as a singer to look after her mother and four siblings.
She recorded her first song in the year 1942 and her first Hindi song in the year
1943. She was a gifted singer with
unprecedented
vocal skills. Once she
established herself as an exceptional singer, there was no looking back for her. In
a career spanning over seven decades, she has lent her voice to countless films in
numerous Indian languages and sung more than 50,000 songs. She also holds the
Guinness World Record for singing the most songs in the world.
She received the highest civilian award of Bharat Ratna in 2001. Due to her*
unmatched voice quality and achievements she was given the title of Swar Kokila
of India. Despite such massive achievements, she stayed humble and was,
completely devoted to music. She passed away on 6th February 2022 but her
songs will stay till eternity.
