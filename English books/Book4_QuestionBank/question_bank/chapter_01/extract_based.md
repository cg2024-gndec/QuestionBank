## Extract Set 1 (Q1–Q4)

**Read the extract and answer the questions:**

*"Once upon a time in China, there lived a boy named Ping. He loved plants and gardening. The Emperor of the land had no children of his own. As he had grown very old, he was worried about the fate of the kingdom. He wanted to find a worthy successor of the crown."*

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH01-EXT-001
- **Type**: Extract Based
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Topic**: Setting & Character
- **Marks**: 3

**Question**: Where did Ping live and what was his hobby?

- **Answer Key**: Ping lived in China. His hobby was plants and gardening — he loved it very much.

### Question 2

- **Type**: Extract Based
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Topic**: Emperor's Problem
- **Marks**: 3

- **Answer Key**: The Emperor was worried because he had no children of his own and he had grown very old. He needed to find a successor to rule the kingdom after him.
