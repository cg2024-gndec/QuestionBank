### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK03_CH10_LA_001
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Narrate the complete content of Chapter 10 ('The Indian Deserts') in your own words.

- **Answer Key**: India has two major deserts. The Thar Desert (Great Indian Desert) is in Rajasthan, covering about 200,000 sq km. It has sand dunes, extreme heat (50°C summer), and cold winters. Plants include cacti and thorny bushes. Animals include camels (Ship of the Desert), desert foxes, and Indian gazelles. The Rann of Kutch in Gujarat is a salt marsh desert, one of the largest salt deserts. It hosts the famous Rann Utsav festival. The White Rann appears as an endless white expanse. Flamingos and wild asses live there.
