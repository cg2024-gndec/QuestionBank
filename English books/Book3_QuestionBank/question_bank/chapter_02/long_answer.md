### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK03_CH02_LA_001
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Narrate the complete story of 'The Jackal and the Dhol' in your own words.

- **Answer Key**: Once upon a time, a king won a long battle fought valiantly against a formidable enemy. To celebrate, he held a grand party with dancers, singers, and musicians. During the celebration, a dhol fell and rolled into the nearby forest. It got stuck in a tree where branches kept hitting it, producing a dham-dham sound.

A jackal passing by heard the sound and observed the dhol from a distance. He wrongly concluded that a soft, delicious animal was trapped inside the dried leather. Excited, he told his friend about the 'tasty animal.' At night, they went together and tore the leather on both sides with great effort. But they found absolutely nothing inside — the dhol was hollow and empty. Disappointed, they realised all their labour was wasted. The moral: Do not act without detailed information.

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: BK03_CH02_LA_002
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Understanding
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Describe the character of the jackal and explain why he is considered foolish.

- **Answer Key**: The jackal is curious, greedy, and impulsive:
- **Curious**: He investigated the strange sound in the forest.
- **Greedy**: He immediately assumed the sound meant food without checking.
- **Impulsive**: He told his friend and tore the dhol apart at night without verifying.
- **Foolish**: He drew conclusions from incomplete evidence (just sound), never tested his assumption, and wasted his entire night's effort for nothing.
