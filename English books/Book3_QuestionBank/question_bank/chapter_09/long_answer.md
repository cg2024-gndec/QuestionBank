### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK03_CH09_LA_001
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Narrate the complete content of Chapter 09 ('Harvest Festival of India') in your own words.

- **Answer Key**: India celebrates many harvest festivals marking the joy of crop harvest. Baisakhi is celebrated in Punjab on April 13/14 marking the wheat harvest and Sikh New Year. Pongal is a four-day Tamil Nadu festival in January celebrating the rice harvest. Onam is celebrated in Kerala in August/September honouring King Mahabali. Makar Sankranti is celebrated on January 14 marking the sun's transition. Bihu is celebrated in Assam marking the harvest season. These festivals unite communities through food, dance, and gratitude.
