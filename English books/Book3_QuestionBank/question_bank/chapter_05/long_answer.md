### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK03_CH05_LA_001
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Narrate the complete content of Chapter 05 ('Invention of Steam Engine') in your own words.

- **Answer Key**: In an English coal mine, water flooding threatened miners. Thomas Newcomen noticed steam from a kettle lifting the lid, inspiring him. After weeks of trial and error he built a machine with boiler, piston, and pump. Steam pushed the piston upwards; cooling made it fall, creating pumping action. It was slow and used too much coal. James Watt later made it more powerful and efficient. Steam engines powered trains, ships, and factories, ushering in the Industrial Revolution.
