### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK03_CH03_LA_001
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Narrate the complete story of 'Two Cats and the Monkey' in your own words.

- **Answer Key**: One day, two friendly cats were playing on the road when they found a roti. They decided to share it equally but started arguing about who got the bigger piece. Unable to resolve the dispute, they asked a monkey sitting on a wall to be their judge.

The cunning monkey had been watching their fight and saw an opportunity. He pretended to compare the pieces, taking a bite from one saying it was bigger, then from the other. He kept repeating this until the entire roti was gone. The helpless cats could only watch. The monkey jumped on the wall and vanished. The cats realised they were foolish to fight and trust a stranger. Moral: Fighting amongst yourselves allows others to take advantage of you.
