Chandrashekhar Azad is one of the most
unforgettable freedom fighters who
contributed immensely to India's
struggle for independence. He inspired
thousands of Indians to join the freedom
movement actively by his exemplary life
and acts of courage.
He was born to Chandra Shekhar Tiwari
on 23rd July, 1906 in Bhavra, Madhya
Pradesh. He grew up alongside the tribal
kids. As a result, he was
incredibly
athletic and actively participated in
sports. He also satisfied his mother's
desire for him to be a Sanskrit scholar by
learning Sanskrit at Kashi Vidyapeeth.
This is where he was first introduced to nationalism.
Friggered by the Jallianwala Bagh massacre, he joined the non-cooperation
movement, led by Mahatma Gandhi. Later on, he joined the Hindustan Republican
Association. To acquire resources for this organization, he organised the Kakori
conspiracy in which the revolutionaries looted weapons and goods from a
government train. He accidentally killed J P Saunders in place of James Scott,
whom he planned to kill to avenge Lala Lajpat Rai. He successfully managed to
escape the authorities which gave him the name ' Quicksilver'
