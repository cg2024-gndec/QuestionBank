civilian honours.

Punjab. He was given the title of FLYING SIKH by general AYUB KHAN of Pakistan, who
was very impressed by his athletic prowess .Singh Is autobiography, The Race of My Life
(co written with his daughter Sonia Sanwalka), was published in 2013. A biopic was made
based on his life titled I Bhaag Milkha Bhaag. 'Singh passed away on June 18, 2021, in
