Harvest festivals are the festivals celebrated to thank nature for a good crop and
to mark the change of seasons. They bring communities together with music,
dance, food and colourful decorations. They are celebrated all over India with
different names in different regions.
Pongal -
It is a four-day harvest festival celebrated in Tamil Nadu in mid-
January. This festival is dedicated to Sun God and Lord Indra ( the God of Rain)
for helping farmers get a good harvest and marks the end of winter solstice.
Kolam( colourful rangoli) adorn every house and special dishes like Sakkarai Pongal
( sweet rice) is prepared. Cattles are worshipped too.
Celebrated on 14thor 15th January every year, Makar Sankranti
