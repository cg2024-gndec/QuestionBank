The Magic of Rain
—Anonymous
1
•
In the sky so dark and grey,
Clouds begin to drift and sway,
A whisper, then a mighty roar,
Rain comes rushing, more and more.
Pitter-patter, drops descend,
On the rooftops, around the bend,
Dancing on the windowpane,
Singing songs in sweet refrain.
The thirsty earth drinks with glee,
Grass grows greener, wild and free,
Puddles form on every lane,
Frogs and ducks come out again.
Children laugh and splash around,
In muddy pools they leap and bound,
Paper boats sail far and wide,
Riding waves with fearless pride.
Then the clouds begin to part,
A rainbow paints a work of art,
Seven colours arch so high,
bridge between the earth and sky.
Oh, the magic of the rain,
Washing away all sorrow and pain,
Leaving behind a world so bright,
Glittering in golden light.
