The Brave Little Kite
—Anonymous
High above in the clear blue sky,
A little kite wanted to fly,
It watched the others dance and soar,
It longed to glide just as before.
It tugged and pulled with all its might,
But fear held it firm and tight,
"What if I fall? What if I fail?"
The little kite began to wail.
The wind whispered soft and kind,
"Be brave, leave doubt behind,
Spread your wings and take the chance,
Trust the wind, join the dance."
With trembling tail and fluttering wings,
The kite let go of fearful strings,
Up it went, twirling high,
Touching clouds in the open sky.
It swooped and swirled, round and round,
Soaring high without a sound,
It laughed and twinkled in the sun,
Realizing flying was so much fun.
From up above the world looked small,
The fields, the trees, the houses tall,
The little kite felt big and strong,
It had found where it belonged.
So, when you're scared to try and fly,
Remember the kite up in the sky,
Word Meaning-
Brave the wind and take the flight,
Glide: To move smoothly without making a noise
You'll find your wings and soar in light.
