The monkey had been sitting there for quite some time and had observed their
fight. He was looking for an opportunity to grab the roti for himself. He readily
agreed to be the judge and lifted the two parts of roti. He pretended to compare
them and took a bite from one portion. "This portion was bigger" , he said. Then he
compared the two parts again and took a bite from the other one saying that the
other portion was bigger. He kept on doing this and the foolish cats could only
look at him helplessly. Within minutes, the monkey gobbled up the full roti and
jumped back on the wall and vanished from there. The cats did not get even a
single bite for themselves. They realised that they were foolish to fight amongst
themselves and trust a stranger.
