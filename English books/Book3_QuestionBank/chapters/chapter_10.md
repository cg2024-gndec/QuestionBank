There are two prominent
arid regions in India with their unique and distinct
charcteristics and cultural significance. The Thar Desert, also known as the Great
Indian Desert, is one of the most prominent deserts in India. It is spread in
Rajasthan, western parts of Gujarat and in Pakistan. It has vast expanses of sand
dunes, rocky terrains, salt flats and barren lands. The region experiences extreme
temperatures in summer with scorching days and freezing nights. Rainfall is
scarce. Drought-resistant flora species like cacti, acacia and thorny bushes and
tough fauna species like desert fox, Indian Gazelle are found here.
The Rann of Kutch is located in Gujarat. It is a salt marsh that remains dry for
most of the year but becomes a shallow lake during the monsoon season. During
