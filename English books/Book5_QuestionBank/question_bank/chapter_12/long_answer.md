### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK05_CH12_LA_001
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Examine the poetic devices used in 'The Magic of Books': Simile, Metaphor, and Imagery.

- **Answer Key**: The poem 'The Magic of Books' uses rich poetic devices to celebrate reading:
1. **Simile**: In the opening line, 'Books are like a magic door', the poet uses 'like' to compare opening a book to stepping through an enchanted door that leads to wonderful places.
2. **Metaphor**: In the final line, 'A treasure house within their mind!', the poet directly equates the human mind to a treasure house without using 'like' or 'as', emphasizing that reading fills the mind with permanent, precious knowledge and ideas.
3. **Visual & Spatial Imagery**: The poet paints vivid mental scenes—'take you to the sky', 'deep below where oceans lie', 'kingdoms, brave and grand', and 'tiny creatures in the sand'—engaging young readers' imagination.

**Question**: Explain why the poet describes a reader's mind as a 'treasure house' and compare it with physical wealth.

- **Answer Key**: The poet describes a reader's mind as a 'treasure house' because reading continuously enriches the mind with valuable knowledge, creative ideas, beautiful memories, and moral wisdom. Comparing this mental treasure house with physical wealth reveals deep truths:

### Question 4
- **Question ID**: BK05_CH12_LA_004
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Describe the structure, rhythm, and rhyme scheme of the poem 'The Magic of Books'.

- **Answer Key**: The poem 'The Magic of Books' is structured thoughtfully for young readers:
1. **Stanzaic Structure**: It consists of 4 four-line stanzas (quatrains), making a total of 16 lines.
2. **Rhyme Scheme**: Every stanza follows an **AABB** rhyme scheme consisting of rhyming couplets:
   - Stanza 1: door/adore (A), thin/within (B)
   - Stanza 2: sky/lie (A), grand/sand (B)
   - Stanza 3: old/untold (A), cry/by (B)
   - Stanza 4: flight/delight (A), find/mind (B)
3. **Rhythm & Musicality**: The regular meter and rhyming couplets create a bouncy, musical rhythm that makes the poem easy to recite, memorize, and appreciate.
