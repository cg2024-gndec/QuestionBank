### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK05_CH01_LA_001
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Write a detailed narrative summary of Chapter 01 ('Sankalp and his Friend').

- **Answer Key**: Sankalp was an unhappy boy in a new town who missed his old school and was bullied by new classmates. One day, escaping bullies, he ran into the woods and got lost. After falling asleep, he woke up in the dark and felt frightened. He met a lost pup, named it Pepper, and asked for its help. The pup sniffed the ground and guided Sankalp out of the woods to his anxious parents and search party. Sankalp declared that he had finally found a true friend.
