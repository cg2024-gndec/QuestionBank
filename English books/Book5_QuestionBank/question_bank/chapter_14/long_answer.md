### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK05_CH14_LA_001
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Examine the descriptions of Spring and Summer in 'The Season's Song'.

- **Answer Key**: The poem provides vivid, sensory descriptions of the first two seasons:
1. **Spring**: Spring opens the annual cycle ('Spring comes first with blossoms fair'). It is characterized by soft green leaves sprouting on trees, warmer air, blooming flowers, and the cheerful humming of bees. The poet describes this season as a time when 'life returns to where it's from', signaling natural rebirth.
2. **Summer**: Summer follows with intense light and warmth ('Then summer shines with golden rays'). It brings long, bright, happy, sunny days. Children enjoy splashing in cool blue waters, while ice cream melts quickly under the hot sun ('one scoop or two!'). Both seasons capture outdoor joy and vibrant life.

**Question**: Examine the descriptions of Autumn and Winter in 'The Season's Song'.

- **Answer Key**: The poem contrasts the cooling second half of the year:
1. **Autumn**: Autumn arrives 'cool and clear' with crunchy fallen leaves covering the ground. The trees undergo a dramatic color transformation, wearing metaphorical 'coats of red and gold'. Days grow shorter and nights turn cold, signaling the approach of winter.

As winter ends, the year completes its journey ('As the year, once more, retires'), preparing for spring's renewal.

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: BK05_CH14_LA_003
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Understanding
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Examine the final two stanzas and explain the 'perfect circle' metaphor for the seasons.

- **Answer Key**: The concluding stanzas deliver the central philosophical theme of the poem:
> *'The seasons sing their song so true,
With something special, old and new.
Each brings magic, joy and cheer—
A perfect circle every year!'*
- **Personification**: The seasons are personified as a harmonious choir singing a true song.
- **Synthesis of Old and New**: Every season combines repeating natural patterns ('old') with fresh new growth and experiences ('new').
- **Perfect Circle Metaphor**: The poet compares the annual cycle of seasons to a 'perfect circle'. A circle has no beginning or end; similarly, as winter finishes, spring immediately begins again. This continuous, unbroken loop reassures us that nature is harmonious, reliable, and filled with joy.

### Question 4
- **Question ID**: BK05_CH14_LA_004
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Describe the structure, rhyme scheme, and poetic devices used in 'The Season's Song'.

- **Answer Key**: The poem 'The Season's Song' is crafted with cheerful poetic elements:
1. **Structure**: It contains 6 stanzas of 4 lines each (quatrains), totaling 24 lines.
2. **Rhyme Scheme**: Every stanza follows an **AABB** rhyme scheme (rhyming couplets):
   - Stanza 1: fair/air (A), hum/from (B)
   - Stanza 2: rays/days (A), blue/two (B)
   - Stanza 3: clear/near (A), gold/cold (B)
   - Stanza 4: blow/snow (A), fires/retires (B)
   - Stanza 5: true/new (A), cheer/year (B)
3. **Poetic Devices**:
   - **Personification**: 'trees wear coats', 'seasons sing their song', 'year retires'.
   - **Metaphor**: 'A perfect circle every year'.
   - **Sensory Details**: Crunchy leaves, golden rays, melting ice cream, frosty air.
