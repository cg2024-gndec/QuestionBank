The Magic of Bboks
- Anonymous
Books are like a magic door,
That takes you places you'll adore.
Through pages thick and pages thin,
A world of wonder lies within.
A book can take you to the sky,
Or deep below where oceans lie.
It shows you kingdoms, brave and grand,
Or tiny creatures in the sand.
You'll meet new friends, both young and old,
Hear stories brave, and tales untold.
With every page, you'll laugh or cry,
Or dream about the days gone by.
So hold a book, and take a flight,
To lands of joy and pure delight.
For those who read will always find,
A treasure house within their mind!
