Very soon Sankalp was out of woods being led on by
the pup. Both of them had gone ahead a little when
they saw a search party heading their way. Sankalp
could see his anxious parents, a few more people
and the school watchman holding a flashlight. The
watchman had seen Sankalp running away in this
direction and shouted after him but Sankalp had
ignored him.
He ran ahead and hugged his parents tightly. He
narrated all that had happened earlier in the day.
Then he said, "This has been the best day so far in
this town but I am sure I have better days ahead.
As you can see Mom, Dad, I have finally made a
friend." He turned back and everybody saw a pup
standing quietly behind Sankalp wagging its tail.
"Meet my friend and saviour Pepper."
