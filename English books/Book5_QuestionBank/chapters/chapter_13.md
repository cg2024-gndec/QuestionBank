Last night I had a funny dream,
I sailed along a chocolate stream.

With marshmallow clouds and jellybean rains.
I met a dragon, green and bright,
But he just smiled, not there to fight.
"Come ride with me!" the dragon said,
So off we flew, high overhead.
We saw the mountains, gold and red,
With cookie crumbs as rocky bed.
The birds were singing songs so sweet,
While cupcakes lined the village street.
But then the sun began to rise,
I woke with wonder in my eyes.
Though dreams may fade with morning's lig
This one will stay from night to night!
So, when you're scared to try and fly,
Remember the kite up in the sky,
