### Page 55

The Season's Song
-Anonymous
Spring comes first with blossoms fair,
Soft green leaves and warmer air.
Flowers bloom and bees will hum,
As life returns to where it's from.
Then summer shines with golden rays,
Long, bright, happy, sunny days.
Children splash in waters blue,
While ice cream melts—one scoop or two!
Next comes autumn, cool and clear,
With crunchy leaves both far and near.
The trees wear coats of red and gold,
As days grow short and nights turn cold.
And last, the winter winds will blow,
With frosty air and falling snow.
We wrap in coats and sit by fires,
As the year, once more, retires.
The seasons sing their song so true,
With something special, old and new.
Each brings magic, joy and cheer—
A perfect circle every year!
