1. Andaman and Nicobar Islands:
11
This group has around 572 islands, but only some are inhabited by people. The capital is
Sri Vijaya Puram, located in the Andaman Islands. These islands are known for their
beautiful beaches, coral reefs and tropical forests. The Cellular Jail, also called Kala
Pani, is a famous historical site here. During the British Raj, our freedom fighters were
held captive here.
The islands are home to many tribes, such as the Jarawa and Sentinelese, who have lived
there for centuries. India's only active volcano is located on Barren Island in the
Andaman sea.
2. Lakshadweep Islands:
This group has 36 coral islands, located in the Arabian Sea. The capital of Lakshadweep
is Kavaratti. These islands are famous for their white sandy beaches, coconut palms and
lagoons and are emerging as tourist hotspot in recent times. The word "Lakshadweep"
means "a hundred thousand islands" in Malayalam.
