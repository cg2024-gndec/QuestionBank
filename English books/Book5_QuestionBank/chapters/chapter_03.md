"Hush baby hush. Don't cry. The hungry tiger will ear you and eat you." Yet again, the
threat fell on deaf ears. This time the tiger felt angry that the child was not terrified of
him. He decided to roar to scare the child. But before he could, the lady said "Hush baby
hush. There is a persimmon (a fruit)". On hearing the name of a fruit the baby fell silent.
The tiger wondered, "What a great animal persimmon must be to scare this brave child
into silence!"
Meanwhile, a thief was waiting to get into the hut, hiding on the slanting thatched roof.
All of a sudden he lost his balance and fell on the tiger's back. The tiger jumped in fright
as he thought that the persimmon had attacked him and started running for his life. The
thief somehow managed to get off the tiger's back and the foolish tiger went back into
the forest.
