### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK02_CH11_LA_001
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Describe the setting, speaker, and main activity in the poem 'A Little Bird I Am'.

- **Answer Key**: In the poem 'A Little Bird I Am', the speaker is a small bird that has been shut away from the open sky ('fields of air') and placed inside a cage. Instead of crying or complaining, the bird sits peacefully in its cage and sings sweet songs all day long. It directs its song toward God, who placed it in the cage, finding complete happiness in singing to please its Creator.

### Question 4
- **Question ID**: BK02_CH11_LA_004
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Understanding
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Explain the old English words used in the poem: 'Thee', 'Naught', 'Doth', and 'wandering'.

- **Answer Key**: 1. **Thee**: An archaic form of 'You', used when praying or speaking reverently to God.
2. **Naught**: An archaic word meaning 'nothing' ('Naught have I else to do').
3. **Doth**: An archaic form of the verb 'Does' ('Doth listen to my song').
4. **Wandering**: In this poem's context, it means free or unconfined ('wandering wing').
