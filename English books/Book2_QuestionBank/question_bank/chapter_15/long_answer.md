**Question**: Explain the steps involved in writing a successful passage composition about a rainy day.

- **Answer Key**: Writing a successful rainy day passage involves five clear steps:
1. **Describe the Weather**: Mention dark clouds, rain falling, and the pitter-patter sound.
2. **Describe Rainwear**: Mention raincoats, gumboots, or umbrellas used to stay dry.
3. **Detail the Fun**: Describe floating paper boats or splashing in puddles.
4. **Describe Nature**: Mention fresh green trees, petrichor scent, or a bright rainbow.
5. **Cozy Ending**: End with returning indoors to dry off and enjoy a warm drink.
