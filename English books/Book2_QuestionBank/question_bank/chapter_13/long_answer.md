### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK02_CH13_LA_001
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Describe the physical appearance, diet, character, and travel habits of the hippopotamus in Chapter 13.

- **Answer Key**: In Arthur Guiterman's poem 'Habits of the Hippopotamus':
1. **Appearance**: The hippopotamus is strong, huge of head, broad of bustle (rear body), and rolls along on big limbs filled with 'hippopotomuscle'.
2. **Diet**: He does not care for sweets like ice cream or pie, preferring food flavored with a little 'hippopotomustard'.
3. **Character**: He is true to his principles, fair ('just'), and always does what he 'hippopotomust'.
4. **Travel Habits**: He never rides in trucks, trams, taxicabs, or omnibuses, which keeps him free from traffic jams and messes.

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: BK02_CH13_LA_002
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Understanding
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Explain the funny invented words used in the poem and what real words they are based on.

- **Answer Key**: The poet Arthur Guiterman invents clever portmanteau words by attaching 'hippopoto-' to everyday words:
1. **Hippopotomuscle**: Hippopotamus + muscle (describing his strong limbs).
2. **Hippopotomustard**: Hippopotamus + mustard (describing his favorite condiment).
3. **Hippopotomust**: Hippopotamus + must (describing his duties).
4. **Hippopotomusses**: Hippopotamus + musses/messes (describing chaotic traffic trouble).
