### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK02_CH07_LA_001
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Describe Sarojini Naidu's birth, family background, and early education.

- **Answer Key**: Sarojini Naidu was born on 13th February 1879 in Hyderabad, British India. She was raised in a highly accomplished family. Her father was a dedicated educationist and social reformer, while her mother was a talented Bengali poet. Growing up in this intellectual home, Sarojini showed remarkable talent early on, writing plays at age 12. Delighted by her genius, her parents sent her to England at age 16 to complete her higher education.

- **Answer Key**: Sarojini Naidu was a gifted poet who possessed a soft, gentle, and melodious voice. During India's struggle for independence, she spoke on numerous public stages about freedom and unity.
