### Question 1
- **Question ID**: BK02_CH10_LA_001
- **Type**: Long Answer
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remembering
- **Marks**: 5

**Question**: Describe the Banyan Tree, including its size, location, and unique root structure.

- **Answer Key**: The Banyan Tree is a huge, majestic tree commonly found in many places across India. It is famous for its unique root structure called aerial roots. These roots grow overhead from the tree's heavy branches, descend down through the air, and penetrate the ground. Once rooted in the soil, they thicken into solid, pillar-like trunks, allowing the Banyan tree to spread further across large areas.

**Question**: How do the aerial roots of a Banyan Tree work to support its massive size?

- **Answer Key**: As a Banyan Tree grows, its heavy horizontal branches extend far outward.
