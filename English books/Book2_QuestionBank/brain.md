# 🧠 Book 2 — Class II English Question Bank Intelligence & Taxonomy

> **Class**: II | **Board**: CBSE / ICSE | **Subject**: English (Language & Literature)  
> **Source Text**: *My Book of English COMPREHENSION & COMPOSITION 2*  
> **Chapters**: 15 | **Questions per Chapter**: 300 (6 categories × 50)

---

## Project Objective

Create a high-quality, exam-aligned Question Bank for Class II English that:
- Mirrors exact CBSE/ICSE Class II English question types (adapted for early primary level)
- Covers every chapter's concepts, characters, vocabulary, phonics, and grammar
- Maintains age-appropriate, clear language for students aged 6–8 years
- Supports Bloom's Taxonomy-based difficulty distribution

---

## 6-Category Question Taxonomy

| # | Category | File | ID Prefix | Marks | Description |
|---|----------|------|-----------|:-----:|-------------|
| 1 | **MCQs** | `mcqs.md` | `CHXX-MCQ-NNN` | 1 | 4-option MCQs: picture/word recognition, basic factual recall, vocabulary, rhyming words |
| 2 | **Fill in the Blanks** | `fill_in_the_blanks.md` | `CHXX-FIB-NNN` | 1 | Sentence completion: missing letters, sight words, basic grammar (is/am/are, a/an/the), chapter facts |
| 3 | **True / False** | `true_false.md` | `CHXX-TF-NNN` | 1 | Simple story-based statements — mark True/False with clear reasons |
| 4 | **Short Answer** | `short_answer.md` | `CHXX-SA-NNN` | 2 | Concise 1–2 sentence responses: character names, simple actions, item listing |
| 5 | **Long Answer** | `long_answer.md` | `CHXX-LA-NNN` | 5 | 3–5 sentence guided responses: picture description, story moral, character feelings |
| 6 | **Extract Based** | `extract_based.md` | `CHXX-EXT-NNN` | 3 | Short 2–4 line text/poem extracts with simple sub-questions: identify speaker, rhyming words, line meaning |

## Difficulty & Bloom's Distribution

| Level | % | Bloom Level | Target Types |
|-------|:-:|-------------|--------------|
| Easy | 50% | Remember, Understand | Direct MCQs, Fill in the Blanks, True/False |
| Medium | 35% | Apply, Understand | Basic grammar fill-ups, Short Answer, extract recall |
| Hard | 15% | Analyze, Evaluate | Guided long answer, moral reasoning, vocabulary in context |
