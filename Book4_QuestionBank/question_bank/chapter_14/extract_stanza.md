## Extract Set 1

**Extract**:
> *"Just a little bit of kindness / Can go a long, long way, / Just a little bit of tenderness / Can brighten up a day."*

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH14-ES-001
- **Type**: ES
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 4
- **Sub-question Label**: (a)
- **Question**: Write a pair of rhyming words from this stanza:
- **Answer Key**: **way** - **day**

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: CH14-ES-002
- **Type**: ES
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 4
- **Sub-question Label**: (b)
- **Question**: What message does the poet give in these four lines?
- **Answer Key**: The poet tells us that **even small acts of kindness and tenderness have a powerful, lasting positive impact**.

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: CH14-ES-003
- **Type**: ES
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 4
- **Sub-question Label**: (c)
- **Question**: List 4 qualities promoted in these lines:
- **Answer Key**: 1. Kindness 2. Tenderness 3. Generosity 4. Positivity

### Question 4
- **Question ID**: CH14-ES-004
- **Type**: ES
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 4
- **Sub-question Label**: (d)
- **Question**: What does 'brighten up a day' mean?
