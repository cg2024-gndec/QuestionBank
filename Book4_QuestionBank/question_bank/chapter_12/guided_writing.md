### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH12-GW-001
- **Type**: GW
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 6
- **Topic**: About River Kaveri
- **Question**: Complete the paragraph using the sentence frames:
  - River Kaveri is a major river in ___.
  - It originates at ___ in the Western Ghats.
  - Its source is called ___.
  - It flows through Karnataka and ___.
  - It falls into the ___ of Bengal.
  - It is known as the Garden of ___.
- **Model Answer**:
  - River Kaveri is a major river in South India.
  - It originates at Brahmagiri Hill in the Western Ghats.
  - Its source is called Talakaveri.
  - It flows through Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.
  - It falls into the Bay of Bengal.
  - It is known as the Garden of Southern India.

### Question 2
- **Question ID**: CH12-GW-002
- **Type**: GW
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 6
- **Topic**: Hydroelectric Power
- **Question**: Complete the paragraph using the sentence frames:
  - Hydroelectricity is produced from ___.
  - On river Kaveri, the ___ Falls generate power.
  - It supplies electricity to cities like ___ and ___.
  - Clean power helps in ___.
  - We should use water carefully because ___.
  - Water is a natural ___.
- **Model Answer**:
  - Hydroelectricity is produced from flowing water.
  - On river Kaveri, the Shivasamudram Falls generate power.
  - It supplies electricity to cities like Bengaluru and Mysore.
  - Clean power helps in lighting homes and running industries.
  - We should use water carefully because it is precious.
  - Water is a natural resource.

### Question 3
