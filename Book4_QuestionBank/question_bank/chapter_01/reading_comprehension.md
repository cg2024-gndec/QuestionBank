## Passage Set 1 (Q1–Q4)

**Read the passage and answer the questions:**

Once upon a time in China, there lived a boy named Ping. He loved plants and gardening. The Emperor of the land had no children of his own. He wanted to find a worthy successor. His messengers travelled all over the empire and announced that the King would choose an heir from amongst the children.

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH01-RC-001
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Bloom Level**: Remember
- **Topic**: Setting
- **Marks**: 2
- **Question**: Where did the story of Ping take place?
  - (A) Japan
  - (B) India
  - (C) China
  - (D) Nepal
- **Answer Key**: (C) China. The passage clearly states: "Once upon a time in China."

### Question 2

  - (B) Cooking
  - (C) Plants and gardening
  - (D) Singing
- **Answer Key**: (C) Plants and gardening. "He loved plants and gardening."

### Question 3
- **Question ID**: CH01-RC-003
- **Type**: RC
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Bloom Level**: Understand
- **Topic**: Vocabulary — successor
- **Marks**: 2

  - (B) A person who comes after another in a role or throne
  - (C) A messenger of the king

- **Answer Key**: (B) A person who comes after another in a role or throne.
