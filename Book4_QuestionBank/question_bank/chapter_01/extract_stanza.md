# Extract & Stanza Analysis — Chapter 01: Empty Pot

---

> **Format**: A short extract from the chapter is given followed by sub-questions:
> (a) A pair of rhyming words (for poems) or two key vocabulary words
> (b) What the author/character wants us to understand
> (c) List 4 details about a character or event
> (d) Meaning of a word from the extract

---

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH01-ES-001
- **Type**: ES
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Bloom Level**: Analyze
- **Topic**: Extract — Ping's Care
- **Marks**: 4
- **Extract**:
  > *"I did, Your Majesty. I changed the soil, watered it regularly, put it out in the sun and air but nothing happened. I am sorry to disappoint you."*
- **Questions**:
  (a) Find **two words** from the extract that show Ping cared for the seed:
  (b) What does Ping want the Emperor to understand through this speech?
  (c) List **4 things** Ping did to care for the seed:
  (d) What does "disappoint" mean?
- **Answer Key**:
  (a) "**watered**" and "**changed**" (also accept: "sun and air")
  (b) Ping wants the Emperor to understand that he **tried his very best with full honesty** but the seed simply would not grow — and he is truly sorry about it.
  (c) 1. Changed the soil 2. Watered it regularly 3. Put it in the sun 4. Gave it fresh air
  (d) "Disappoint" means to **make someone feel let down or sad** because something did not turn out as expected.
