# Guided Writing -- Chapter 15: A Child's Thought of God

> **Format**: 6-sentence guided paragraph/description frames. 50 tasks.

---

### Question 1
- **Question ID**: CH15-GW-001
- **Type**: GW
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Marks**: 6
- **Topic**: Thinking About God and Nature
- **Question**: Complete the paragraph using the sentence frames:
  - God's glory shines in ___.
  - When I look at the sky, I see ___.
  - The trees and flowers show ___.
  - God's love is gentle like ___.
  - I feel peaceful when ___.
  - Nature teaches us that God is ___.
- **Model Answer**:
  - God's glory shines in all created things.
  - When I look at the sky, I see beautiful clouds and stars.
  - The trees and flowers show the beauty of nature.
  - God's love is gentle like a mother's warm hug.
  - I feel peaceful when I sit silently in nature.
  - Nature teaches us that God is everywhere around us.
