idea for developing traffic signals began in the 1800s when
people either walked or were driven around on horse-drawn
carriages. On December 10, 1868, the first gas-lit traffic
lights were installed outside the Houses of Parliament in
London. It was proposed by a British railway engineer, J.P
Knight. This was installed to control the traffic of horse
carriages in the area and to allow pedestrians to safely cross the
roads. The electric traffic lights, were invented by Garrett Morgan in
1923. This marked a significant step forward in traffic management.
