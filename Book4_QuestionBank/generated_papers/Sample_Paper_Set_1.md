# ANSWER KEY – CLASS IV ENGLISH SAMPLE PAPER (SET 1)

## SECTION A – READING (16 Marks)

### Q1. Passage 1 ("The Empty Pot")
- **(A)** Correct Answer: **(B) To choose the next Emperor of China** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: The Emperor gave seeds to all children so that whoever showed their best in a year would succeed him to the throne.
- **(B)** Correct Answer: **(B) Deep sadness or grief** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: 'Sorrow' means deep sadness when nothing grew in Jun's pot despite watering it daily.
- **(C)** Correct Answer: **(B) Honesty** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: The opposite of dishonesty is honesty.
- **(D)** Correct Answer: **(C) Beautiful** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: 'Beautiful' is an adjective, whereas planted, watered, and embraced are action verbs.

### Q2. Passage 2 (Unseen Narrative)
- **(A)** Correct Answer: **(B) To the green park near their house** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Rohan and Bruno went for a morning walk in the green park.
- **(B)** Correct Answer: **(A) Its wing was caught in dry twigs near the water edge** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Rohan saw the baby bird struggling because its wing was trapped in dry twigs.
- **(C)** Correct Answer: **(B) Bright yellow** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Bruno was chasing a bright yellow butterfly.
- **(D)** Correct Answer: **(B) Rohan's Kind Rescue** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: The story describes how Rohan kindly rescued a baby bird in the park.
