## SECTION A – READING (16 Marks)

### Q1. Passage 1 ("Boat Races Festival Kerala")
- **(A)** Correct Answer: **(B) Vallam Kali, meaning "boat game"** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Vallam Kali translates to 'boat game' in local Malayalam.
- **(B)** Correct Answer: **(C) A single massive tree trunk** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Chundan Vallams are traditionally hand-carved from a single massive tree trunk.
- **(C)** Correct Answer: **(B) Maritime** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Maritime means connected with the sea, rivers, navigation, and ships.
- **(D)** Correct Answer: **(C) Uniforms** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: 'Uniforms' is a noun (clothing item), whereas Rowers, Spectators, and Chanting refer to people or actions.

### Q2. Passage 2 (Unseen Narrative)
- **(A)** Correct Answer: **(A) Under the mango tree in her garden** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Ananya found the injured squirrel lying under the mango tree.
- **(B)** Correct Answer: **(B) He cleaned its wound and fed it apple slices and water with a dropper** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Grandfather cleaned the wound and nourished the squirrel with fruit and water.
- **(C)** Correct Answer: **(B) Full of energy and active** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: Energetic means active and full of life after recovering.
- **(D)** Correct Answer: **(B) Ananya and the Injured Squirrel** *(2 Marks)*  
  *Explanation*: The passage focuses on how Ananya saved and cared for an injured squirrel.
